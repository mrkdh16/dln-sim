DLN Widget Test Bed

Each widget is self-contained — just a <div> and a factory call.

Widget 1 · Linear vs Nonlinear

Preset cards drive an independent simulation. Shows loss and W₁ singular values.

Widget 2 · Singular Value Theory

E2e singular values vs Saxe et al. theory curves. Depth selector auto-sets lr and init scale.

Widget 3 · Loss Curve

Loss over training. Compare plateaus across depths; adjust target scale to see sharper drops.

Widget 4 · Strong Alignment

Three simultaneous runs: lazy (σ₀=2.0), middle (σ₀=0.3), rich (σ₀=0.005). Heatmaps show |U₂ᵀU|, |V₁ᵀV|, |V₂ᵀU₁| — blue diagonal = strongly aligned, white off-diagonal = decoupled. Drag the slider to scrub through the training trajectory.
